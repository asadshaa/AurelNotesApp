# Aurel Design System & Aesthetic Philosophy

Aurel follows an **Editorial Warm Minimalist** design aesthetic inspired by high-end literary magazines and modernist notebook stationery.

## Palette Principles
- **Warm Canvas (`#F8F6F1`):** Soft cream paper tone that reduces glare and eye strain.
- **Deep Obsidian Ink (`#25232B`):** Readable contrast without harsh pure black.
- **Editorial Violet (`#7564D8` / `#9C8DF6`):** Intentional accent color reserved for interactive highlights.
- **Card Pastels:** Subtle desaturated pastels (Lavender, Sage, Peach, Sand, Mist Blue) for cognitive categorization.
